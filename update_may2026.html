May 2026 Godot 4 Still Alive Solo Dev

Apologies for the quiet month. Diablo IV released its second DLC at the end of April, and I have been conducting um... thorough market research. I have logged many hours in service of understanding the "competition" and I regret nothing.

Okay, I regret a little. The project did not move much in May. But I'm back now, and if anything I'm more motivated than before.

What the "Research" Turned Up

One of the things that genuinely impressed me about Lord of Hatred was how Blizzard handled the endgame progression. I hadn't seriously played a season since Vessel of Hatred, and the amount that has changed in that time caught me off guard. The endgame now has clear tracks, structured paths for building out your character that give you something to focus on without making the whole thing feel overwhelming.

The endgame structure gave me some ideas. The systems in this game are very different but I want to make sure I give the players something to work toward and make sure they know what it is.

The story was also pretty good. Nothing groundbreaking, but it moved along and was entertaining to the end. I think as long as I tell an interesting story that doesn't get in the way of the gameplay I will have done my job!

Back to Designing

This is just a quick note, no major changes or announcements, just wanted to keep everyone informed that I'm still working on the game!

Next time, I'll get into the games that inspired me (you'll never guess what game is on that list).
